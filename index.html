About

Saroj Khanal is an independent researcher and physics educator based in Nepal. His work spans computational and theoretical approaches to problems in astrophysics, materials science, and molecular modelling. Alongside research, he teaches physics and supports students in developing scientific reasoning and research skills.

This website provides an academic overview and links to scholarly records. Publication details should be checked against the linked publisher records.

Research interests

Computational astrophysics

Galactic dynamics, fuzzy dark matter, and computational modelling of astrophysical systems.

Energy-storage materials

Lithium-ion battery ageing, variability, and thermodynamic–stochastic modelling.

Computational molecular modelling

Molecular structure, geometric constraints, and computational analysis of ligand systems.

Physics education

Teaching physics and mentoring student-led scientific inquiry.
